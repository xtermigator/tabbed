# My Tab Hub Pro — Privacy Policy

My Tab Hub Pro processes browser information locally to organize tabs and help you find saved or previously visited websites.

## Information used
- Open tab titles and URLs for search, suggested workspace categories and tab controls.
- Browser history titles, URLs, visit counts and last-visit times when history is enabled. The extension reads up to 1,500 records from the last 90 days into memory.
- Favorites, saved sessions, workspace names and URL assignments, preferences and layout stored in the browser profile using chrome.storage.local.

The extension does not read full webpage contents, passwords, payment information, or website form entries. It includes no analytics, advertising, remote code, account system or external AI service. Extension data is not sent to the developer or sold or shared with third parties.

History and suggestions are enabled initially. You can disable them in Settings. The history setting stops history reads and clears the current in-memory history list; it does not remove the installation-time history permission or delete the browser's own history. Favorites and saved sessions remain until removed or until extension data is removed. Uninstalling normally removes extension-local data. There is no cross-browser sync or export facility.

Selecting Reopen or restoring a session instructs the browser to visit the selected website. Those websites and the browser have their own privacy practices. This policy covers the extension only.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions may be reported through the repository issue tracker: https://github.com/xtermigator/tabbed/issues . Do not include private browsing data in a public issue.
